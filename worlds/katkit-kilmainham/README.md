# KatKit Kilmainham · R001

The Asylum of Disputed Things: a fictional former abbey, court, and political prison in year 417 of the Silver Regency. Eight connected nodes, five decisions, three consequence axes, a conditional council ending. Routes cost 2 fictional minutes; choices cost 3. Local persistence, reset only this world. Open index.html directly. No portal yet.

## Direction
Kilmainham Gaol and the separate-system prison inspire the architecture, not actual inmates or medical history. Literary lenses: The Count of Monte Cristo (wrongful confinement), Les Misérables (law and mercy), Darkness at Noon (political confession), The Trial (procedure), The Crucible (testimony), Crime and Punishment (conscience). Film grammar: The Shawshank Redemption (institutional scale), The Lives of Others (listening and conscience), Shutter Island (uncertain interpretation), The Passion of Joan of Arc (faces and institutional framing). Original fictional scenarios; no copied scenes or dialogue, no historical research claimed. Psychological tension is expressed through perspective, evidence, relationships and consequences.
