## The Choir
The event ledger is authoritative. An opportunity or visitor testimony produces local receipts. A power chooses gift, vice or reserve through accumulated disposition, momentum, a bounded event-specific ethical probe and independent seeded variation. Local human witnesses accept, challenge or reserve, producing causal replies. Forms change independently of polarity. Adjacent-node migration is separate from player navigation. Historical snapshots preserve prior trajectories. Rendering merely reads committed public expressions. No universal moral score, no hidden-thought narration, no real-person or religious classification. The three experimental probes (evidential honesty, voluntary agency, livable futures) are NOT the canonical Abrahamic Triangle; its vertices and exact seven-way tournament assignment remain unresolved and are not silently fabricated. Sable's gift remains sealed. The current implementation samples four fictional historical occasions, not a calibrated civilization simulation. Public short lines are authored, not LLM-generated. Future work: richer authored events, delayed human commitments, independent messages, spatially placed speech, real animation and persistent long-run history.
