# Miss Beatrice Finch — The Eleven Titles of Departures
Canon supplement, 26 November 2026. User accepted the character and every moniker; NPCs have all the power. Source: [R011 lore](LORE_R011.md), [R012 scope](R012_SCOPE.md). This supplement does not replace the seven powers, fix the Triangle, or alter historical epochs.

## Character
ID `human.beatrice_finch`; 58; case manager, former theatrical wardrobe mistress. Plum jacket, silk scarf, tortoiseshell spectacles, a man's watch, immaculate shoes, overflowing desk. Resourceful, vain, socially perceptive, affectionate, impatient, intrusive. Her blind spot is imagining a future for someone which they do not want. She can apologize and be corrected; she is not omniscient. She remembers hopes rather than offenses. She is human, not an eighth power. Her connection to Administrative Magic is enacted through accumulated documents and unrealized futures, not magical dialogue. She does not explain, hint at or name the magic. Her own knowledge is not established. The mid-century appearance is an art stratum, not a silent rewrite of the 1879 epoch.

## The inmate titles
Every title is affectionate. The residents want her assistance intensely but preserve dignity through comic ceremony. No title is sarcastic by default, no title is a password, no title increases trust or unlocks magic. Respect individual variation: a quiet resident need not participate. No one is entitled to her affection or labor, and care creates no debt.

- **The Duchess of Departures and Unfortunate Returns** — returning resident.

## NPC sovereignty
NPCs have all the narrative and institutional power. Player has agency over their own actions, not other agents: approach, offer, observe, sit, wait, leave, and manipulate accessible objects under world rules. NPCs initiate, allocate turns, choose topics, interrupt, decline to engage, invite company, leave, authorize or withhold institutional permissions, and pursue independent schedules. No player-facing persuasion options, dialogue quiz, success/failure label, completion flag, affection meter, guaranteed rewards or forced consent. Repeated approach never farms trust or forces a branch. Nothing said by Finch authorizes a case, even if reassuring. Ordinary care and legally necessary boundaries remain legible; urgent practical requests get appropriate ordinary attention, not whimsical dismissal as a puzzle. NPC silence is not punishment. Player can leave at any beat. Inmates also exercise independent power and can refuse Finch. Conversations happen while the visitor is absent and can be overheard only locally.

## Living conversation tree
At a material occasion, select an eligible NPC-owned scene. The speaker chooses to speak, pause, turn to another NPC, return to work, or leave. An independent actor may interrupt. NPCs may choose no branch. Branches are possibilities, not buttons or a sequence of correct moves. Every beat has an ordinary exit; no UI verdict. Use schedules, observable facts, witnessed receipts and independent RNG. Keep budget short (1–4 beats in one exchange; longer exchanges arise from new occasions), preserve consent and spatial audibility. Never narrate a real or fictional character's private thoughts as facts. Rendering reads committed public events only.

## Administrative Magic: the office of unrealized futures
Dialogue does not trigger it. Typewriter, stamps, carbon copies, registers, keys, requests, letters, appointment ledgers, filing cabinets and discharge certificates have stable IDs, custody, provenance, scope and history. Player may offer or rearrange permitted objects. The resulting claim is not true or approved merely because it is written. NPC-owned authority creates permissions and actual appointments via committed object events. Preserve duplicate records and superseded versions. A filing can produce a candidate opportunity, never a guaranteed outcome. The world changes through documented conditions and independently chosen authorized acts; no NPC speech as spell, password or direct state mutation. Finch never says that the visitor passed or failed. A returned resident's envelope, a canceled bakery interview and an authorized filing can create a renewed opportunity; the employer and resident retain the power to refuse. An unsigned certificate is not release. A numbered key is not a right to enter. Effects can occur offscreen or later, without notification. Scientific experiments remain independent, plausible and do not receive magical alterations or hidden outcomes.
