4. **Addressable architecture.** 138 visual door addresses (46 bays × 3 tiers). Exactly one open: east:t1:right:06, a deliberate local threshold to east:cell-06. Every closed leaf inert; no speculative portal destinations. Future child-world exits use explicit opt-in public arrival tags, separate clocks and source allowlist, never arbitrary URLs or private state.

Archive and Undercroft receive stable addresses, authored junction/vestibule geometry and reserved doors first; no claim they are playable until a complete round trip and interaction exists. Each district eventually has its own silhouette, lighting, sound, local encounter, historical trace, meaningful optional choice and safe return. No fictional occult explanation for real history. Do not add arbitrary collection locks.

Existing Choir node-relative interpolation is not collision/pathfinding; replace it with actor schedule and nav adapters, support-aware foot placement, facing/turning, wait/sit/walk and optional compatible animation clips after inspection. Build one disputed exchange with three actual vantage positions (court, gallery, obstructed station), one absent observer, contradictory receipts and append-only correction. Distinguish observation, report, interpretation and private intent. Source position, scope, modality, range, occlusion, permitted audience and clock matter. The candidate occurrence module is not a replacement for Choir or a security boundary; it is an adapter proposal that needs reconciliation with existing IDs/receipts and tests. Use actual geometry visibility, not a boolean invented from proximity.

## Acceptance gates
A: original ground route/testimony works; no R010 startup regression; real browser frame and optional asset-failure mode.
B: human calibration camera and body bounds, stair headroom across entire swept capsule, supported bridge and catwalk, clear entrance/exit; sample all route centerlines <=.15m and swept body at multiple heights, assert wall/door collisions, reverse paths, no rail intersections. Test actual geometry and dynamic blockers, not merely this candidate contract. Step count from 3D length and actual elapsed travel.
C: one open cell with complete interior and return; 137 closed addresses non-traversable; inspect hoist and screen clearances; no fake portal.
D: West Wing and Cloister real reversible threshold routes, one independently initiated human choice in each; Archive/Undercroft reserved unless complete. No dead ends that trap the player.
E: one multi-view incident, distinct modalities and absence, one correction and report not equated with direct sight, no private letter leakage, independent actor action while away, same ledger with presentation disabled. A memory visit leaves facts untouched. Full direct tests for Choir unchanged plus new adapter.
F: Actual Chromium/WebGL browser path from intake to open cell and return, frame/texture timings, screenshot evidence with standing person and canopy, keyboard/touch, safe mode, deployment URL and served commit. No claim of pass from syntax alone.
