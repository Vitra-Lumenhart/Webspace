# KIL-IRON-003 — The food hoist
26 November 2026 · reference-driven R012 implementation increment

## Evidence, then interpretation

Primary: Kilmainham Gaol Museum, OPW/Blackrock Teachers' Centre, [Primary School Pack](https://www.kilmainhamgaolmuseum.ie/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/Primary-School-Pack.pdf), Gaol Trail Stop 2. It places the food lift on the right of the centre stairs and identifies two exposed vertical poles, an overhead pulley and a floor trapdoor to the kitchen. Its illustration shows a round pulley, decorated horizontal support and diagonal line. Food came up from the kitchen; a warder and two prisoners distributed it, beginning on the ground floor and then serving the two upper levels. The cell-door food flap folded down to make a shelf. Diet examples include oatmeal and milk, bread and vegetable soup.

Corroboration: [National Inventory of Architectural Heritage, East Wing](https://www.buildingsofireland.ie/building-of-the-month/kilmainham-gaol-inchicore-road-kilmainham-dublin-8/) identifies three-tier food delivery by pulley. [Museum photograph, food lift at left from reverse view](https://www.kilmainhamgaolmuseum.ie/kilmainham-gaol-view-of-east-wing-with-food-lift-on-left/); [right-side photo caption](https://www.theirishplace.com/heritage/history-kilmainham-gaol/); [wide view with slender poles, Geograph](https://www.geograph.ie/photo/7877959); [axial Wikimedia view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dublin-Kilmainham-Jail-Hall-1.jpg). The wide views were inspected; the museum full-resolution image and the PDF screenshot were not retrievable. Do not assert surveyed dimensions or exact historic carrier, rope reeving, hatch hinge, support detail or maintenance practice.

## Shape priorities

Replace the earlier proposed cage tower with an exposed two-pole hoist. Thin blue-grey iron poles, decorated crosspiece/brace, visible circular wheel, thin hauling rope, plain shallow food carrier, floor hatch. No motor, automatic passenger doors, full-height four-post cage, passenger elevator or fantasy embellishments. The game model uses a 1.12 m pole separation, 14.25 m head height and a .76 × .65 m carrier scaled to the approved fictional hall. A simple carrier, pulley spokes, rope path, hatch pivot and small gallery service ledges are interpretive. Confirm against closer photography before final art acceptance. Original geometry lives in food-hoist-view.js, with separately named moving groups. No exported GLB is claimed yet.

## Ordinary life first

KIL-IRON-003 has its own clock, identity, custody and work history, not human psychology or membership in the seven powers. The initial ambient meal round starts after three simulation seconds; the carrier ascends from a kitchen recess to ground, first gallery and second gallery, pauses to hand off one abstract batch per tier, then returns empty. No teleportation. Wheel rotation and cable length follow the tray. Subsequent rounds are requested through the local inspection marker at hall or chapel. Food recipients/warder/helpers are not animated in this increment. Gallery service ledges are scenery only until the vertical traversal and screen openings are validated.

## Maintenance as institutional history

The machine is a recurring character through the human care invested in it. Wear accrues with travel distance; completed rounds, custody transfers and service requests are retained in its session ledger. When service is due, the next round waits; service is available only empty and at rest. Simulated service reduces wear and records a dated tick without deleting past events. Do not present this as real machinery guidance. Future design: distinct inspections, condition and cleaning vs component replacement; repair parts with provenance, recurring maintenance staff, audible differences before/after care, postponed rounds and alternative delivery, independently initiated work orders, enduring object biography across epochs. No forced starvation, random injury, or punitive player trap. Keep a food delivery and maintenance event distinct from witnessed evidence: only actual observers gain Choir receipts.
