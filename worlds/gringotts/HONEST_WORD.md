## Authoring contract
The Handshake becomes a constrained discourse interpreter, not an LLM or natural-language authorization service. Frame = subject/predicate/object + provenance + scope; intent = ask/offer/refuse. Each scene gives two authored voices, a physical gesture, a Thousand analogy, three choices and an always-available exit. No implicit player agreement; no score, debt, registration or punishment for refusal. A request is not a transaction. Invitation → choose → terms for effects → confirmation/decline → model outcome → leave. Versioned controls reject stale requests; reset increments version. Only a closed effect vocabulary can change demo state. The model, not character prose, decides outcome. Future LLM can rephrase approved semantic frames only; external authoritative service independently validates all actions. Speech and emotes are never proof of permission.

## Gold-standard discourse
Distinguish observation, testimony, inference, hypothesis, public rule, synthetic action and proposal. State recipient, purpose, duration, scope, consequence, revocation, alternative. Distinguish secret content from metadata, person from account, possession from title, valid signature from truth, consent from access, request from confirmed effect, local writ from bank grant. No automatic cross-world identity linking. No penalties for anonymity or declining a bank account. A public record is not a private dossier. Scope witness/GM knowledge separately. Respect independent Court, Last Lantern, Mara, Blackwater and Hall of Uncertain Arts.
