Ledger and Seal — technical manual

In-memory model with BigInt amounts serialized as decimal strings. Private closure stores accounts, grants, journal, escrows and estate status. Initial reserve is 1,000,000 toy chips. Authorization moves reserve to a held grant; redemption moves held credit to world budget; awards, transfers, escrow, refund and succession preserve the total. Grant IDs, audience and expiry checked; world adapters expose only their own modeled scope. Caller roles are not real authentication; any browser user controls the runtime. The expiry clock is a supplied synthetic tick. No real cryptography or persistence; do not deploy as a service.

Worlds may independently implement their own economies. Witness gets copies of redacted events. Estate close transfers remaining committed balance to a succession account; outstanding escrow remains governed by its contract (future estate/escrow policy required). No external account exists. No real credentials, money, or external transactions. The separate toy purse yard models local theft and restitution using purely fictional state, no bank authority.
