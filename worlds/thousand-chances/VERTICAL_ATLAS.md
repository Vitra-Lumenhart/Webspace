# TC-D03 / TC-R0007 — The Veins and the Hanging City
26 November 2026 · exterior sculpting, no secondary worlds

## Canon and settlement history
Keep the original 72 immutable building records and all 25 original anchors. The original 8 cultural weights (ordinary 42%, Carnival 19%, Reclaimers 10%, Caravan of Stories 8%, Masquerade 7%, Commoners 6%, Quiet Society 5%, Cyberpunk 3%) and 60 actors are preserved. Anonymous remains cross-cutting, not a census category. House placement and clothing do not determine beliefs, conduct or permissions. The city is inherited trade infrastructure: lower commercial settlement → inhabited cliffs → hanging villas → mixed contemporary districts. The placement below is authored, deterministic exterior sculpting, not a running settlement simulation.

Hearth Galleries: ordinary households favor routes, services and shared amenities. Ribbon Heights: Carnival makes bridges into gathering places and spreads public decorations. Western Grotto: Reclaimers reuse inherited anchors and workshops; Caravan of Stories preserves old thresholds and histories. Velvet Cliffs: Masquerade and Carnival experiment with windows, textile facades and individual silhouettes. Oven Terraces: Commoners favor shared water and affordable links. Blue Recesses / Eastern Fault: Quiet Society values shelter and acoustic separation. Crown Signal Nest: one rare technological home with disproportionate infrastructure influence. Mixed galleries reflect migration, inheritance and independent preferences. Height is not status. Shrine motifs and religious accessories do not determine affiliation.

## Eight exterior objectives
1. Arrival terrace: union of coplanar raised route polygons and landing pads (Shapely constrained triangulation); jittered Voronoi repair patches in arrival region, subtle lime aggregate shader, no old painted stripes. Dedicated overlook at [0,30,35], 1.7m eye height, slender rail. Existing kiosks and route choices preserved.
2. Asymmetric central dome: 96 azimuth samples, 25 vertical rings and asymmetric harmonic expansion, crown 112m; preserve original market, wheel, waterfall and 72 building records. Six actual shell openings with interpolated rock collars.
3. The Veins: eight lower Silk Vein junctions in a closed loop, eight upper House Vein junctions in a separate loop, four Lost Vein switchbacks between them. External radius 145m, Lost nodes 190m. North/South gates, Western Grotto, Eastern Fault and two Crown reentries. Swept arches, widened junction chambers, floor skirts and warm lamps. Separate from original Belowroads puzzle and no actual external-world portal. No stealth mechanic yet.
4. Six public hearth galleries (one for each inherited row) and 18 new closed-door landings. Every original residence has an address record and planned gallery, but individual door spurs for original 72 are deferred; not every inherited door is reachable. No entry or interiors.
5. Eighteen new authored residences: Western Grotto 4, Velvet Cliffs 4, Eastern Fault 3, Crown 3, mixed upper galleries 4. Species totals 5 Lantern, 4 Grotto, 3 Weaver, 2 Conservatory, 3 Bridge, 1 Signal. New floor heights 44,46.5,60,62.5,76,78.5m. Beams and ties extend toward sampled dome wall; fictional supports, not real structural calculations. Closed thresholds and windows, differentiated roofs, flowerboxes and banners. Geometry is stylized, no private scene or inventory.
6. Domestic ornament: glass panes, flowerboxes, textiles, support ties, hanging structures, and sheltered gallery architecture. Full water/hoist utility simulation, shrines and sophisticated accumulated histories remain future work.
7. Junction furniture and small counter props at selected Silk/House/Lost junctions; empty performance rugs where clear. Prior KayKit CC0 small prop geometry reused. No additional people or active events. Original 20 authored market offers unchanged; new tables are decorative and not yet interactive merchants.
8. Route validation: 77 nodes and 100 documented links, 92 open, 8 closed (6 historic, 2 new shortcuts: ribbonWalk:mixedHigh and hearthW1:hearthW2). All destinations reachable and returnable. 18,136 static samples at <=0.25m spacing, zero sampled body hits and unsupported centers for seed 365. No continuous collision, dynamic collision or full visual clearance at every pixel. Narrow rock seams and primitive roof/rail modeling still visible. 22 native GLES views passed, Chromium CPU spawn/entry walk and camera passed, no GPU browser or audio verification. Full hosted preview and all-route browser traversal not verified.
