# TC-D01 — Cultural Atlas of the Thousand Chances
Approved 26 November 2026; first implementation TC-R0003. Gringotts is external: designed order, verified settlement and preservation versus emergent order, negotiated opportunity and reinvention. Mos Eisley-like heterogeneity and Carnival decoration remain dominant. Cyberpunk is the smallest resident affiliation and the loudest technological presence. Population, territory, attention, and infrastructure are independent. Resident weights are proposed casting targets, not measured census; primary culture is independent from occupation, identity, ability, wealth, private association and technology use.

| Primary culture | Weight | Cast of 60 | Material vocabulary | Cultural practice and future play |

Anonymous is an overlapping, sometimes collective, public identity, not a ninth population; no public census or individual membership inference. Witness, Satirist, Invisible Citizen, Dissident, Archivist, Opportunist are narrative roles, not criminal or technical permissions. Public unsigned statements label opinion vs evidence. No faction uniform or automatic alignment. Do not equate artificial beings with Cyberpunk. Include elderly, children, temporary residents, night workers, disabled and solitary inhabitants, people without capital, and eventually artificial beings across all affiliations; current low-poly cast does not yet implement these dimensions.

## Cultural geography
Last Lantern: mixed travelers, quiet and storytellers; Balcony: guides and Carnival art; Bazaar: ordinary people and Carnival with conspicuous tech screens; Second Names: Masquerade and tailors; Broken Things: Reclaimers and hardware; Uncertain Arts: stories, inventors and outsiders; Small Fortunes: Carnival and performance; Commons: Commoners and everyone; Court: diverse institutions and mediators; Quiet Market: restrained artisans and readers; Blackwater: laborers, traders, Reclaimers; Houses Above: mixed ordinary residents. No hard cultural borders. Retain cave approach and return only, tiered housing, string lights, player-owned camera, optional atlas, no automatic pickpockets.

## Accretion / character evolution
Actor metadata primary affiliation, coat and trim, role, home and route; deterministic seeded shuffle and group-biased routes. Future: secondary affiliations, self-authored identity, age, personal interests, jobs, activity schedules, actual relationships, memory, finite inventory, multiple distinct sensory profiles and independent offscreen activity. Named actor continuity via Traveling Company, no duplication or forced association. Culture informs attire/props and attractions, never determines skills or ethics. Carnival procession and portable story performance should later become dynamic events. Quiet Market suppresses nearby calls and attenuates synthetic ambience; not a measured acoustic model. The current story cloth, civic note, costumes and quiet frontage are blockout props. No complete interiors, high-poly assets, actor-specific voice or actual economic simulation. Native textures and geometry tested; browser blocked in this environment. Player can ignore every advertisement.
