# TC-D02 — The Silk Roads and the Inhabited Terraces
26 November 2026 · TC-R0004 / proposed Webspace R0028

An old trading city does not end at its central market. Commercial opportunities follow the routes people repeatedly take. Entrances grow provisions and resting places; departure routes grow last-minute goods; neighborhoods grow bodegas and quiet bookstores. The original city geometry remains authoritative, and all 72 generated building records are identical to TC-R0003. The new decoration is an accreted surface layer, not a relocation or demolition.

## Eight architectural influences (not a census)
Hearth Rows — ordinary households, warm plaster, planters and practical lamps. Ribbon Terraces — Carnival pennants, warm gold, bright cloth. Menders' Ledges — repaired timber, old shutters and patch plates. Painted Cloth Houses — storytellers' fabric banners, faded blue and red. Velvet Galleries — Masquerade shutters, pink and mauve details. Oven Terraces — Commoner practical planters, communal warmth. Blue Alcoves — subdued blue-green shutters and cool interior lamps. Little Signal House — a single Cyberpunk residential facade with cyan signal strips. Mixed-frontage rules add cloth ornament and blend neighboring styles on 16 of 72 façades; these are decorative assignments, not resident counts. All original footprints, heights, roof configurations and structural supports retained. Anonymous remains a crosscutting social affiliation, never a house color.

These are preliminary stations, not detailed shop interiors or street performance simulation.

## Cave Lite

The source density field uses elliptical air, broad harmonic relief, geology, protected route, and indexed marching tetrahedra on a 111×75×157 grid. TC-R0004 retains elliptical air and two harmonic relief terms, protected corridor, and radial bisection zero crossings; replaces voxel volume with 64 angular samples × 9 dome rings and two 9×17 tube profile grids. Surface is single-valued; no branches inside the geological surface, general implicit topology, or metric SDF. Open city volume remains, entrances have flared irregular mouths. World-space bedding, seams and warm canopy glow use analytic shading, no added texture maps. Original Wheel and lights retained. Static GPU attributes packed in 24-byte stride (float32 position, normalized int16 normals, normalized uint8 RGBA; 2 padding bytes), previously 40-byte stride. Dynamic wheel and existing crowd stay 40-byte.
