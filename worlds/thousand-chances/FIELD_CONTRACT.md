# TC-R0011 / R0035 — The Field of Small Consequences
26 November 2026. First integrated implementation of TC-D05. No additional worlds, no external financial connection.

## Player guide
The City button opens an optional record of the local economy, last six incidents, restitution, portable save export/import and Vitra's five field sliders. The city runs while visible; no cookies. Personal fictional encounters are OFF by default. Enable them in City if desired; the first automatic opportunity is at least ten active simulated minutes after startup, with a ten-minute minimum cooldown. The optional encounter arises only during road travel near an active fictional sale. It is a stylized Xenos movement contest: choose half-step, commit or retreat, alternate direction to evade, or guard/withdraw safely. The player never loses real value, credentials, or Gringotts assets; a failed optional encounter moves 12 local chips into disputed custody. Walk to the Court of Many Promises and use City → Request restitution. Backtrack/Escape withdraw safely. Camera orientation is never taken over. NPC activity is visible independently of personal opt-in. Static background extras retain their artwork; temporary actors are not persistent NPCs. The Atlas practice remains intact.

## Road Fabric and surface grammar
The immutable 77 anchors, 92 open connections, 90 addresses and 98 authored offers remain. `road-fabric.js` subdivides the 4448.45 m authored centerlines into 2264 cells at <=2 m; adjacency follows endpoints, not proximity through rock or different elevations. `compile-sockets.py` uses the exported static triangles to validate two standing positions for each of 92 ground-level shops: supported foot height and 0.31 m clearance at four body heights. It does not prove paths into those positions or general dynamic collision. Six offers have no activity sockets and are inspection-only. Existing sampled routes still support centerline travel. `render.js` material-tags civic lime, residential slope timber and mineral surfaces; flat unioned terraces use lime. Static triangle centroid maps to closest road cell within 9 m. Packed vertex stride remains 24 bytes, with a uint16 in previous padding: `4*(cell+1)+material`, 0 for untagged, material 1 lime / 2 timber / 3 mineral. GPU samples a 2264x1 RGBA8 road memory texture, with slow commercial residue influencing polish; CPU approximates grain and residue. No arbitrary gold stripes. Current geometric roads and rails retained, not a full-width deck reconstruction. A material shader is not a collision or friction solver. World geometry is partitioned by 24 m centroid tiles; conservative sphere frustum culling submits ranges in the shared buffer, not streaming.

## Numerical and dramatic contract
`field.js` holds seven Float32 values per road cell (C commerce, A attention, O opportunity, T trust, G grievance, W prosperity, R restoration). Three bounded residual arrays store local / road / road-class impressions, with 4/40/360-second base half-lives and 2-second graph diffusion updates. At an event, the local cell, its road with exponential spatial falloff and road-class aggregate receive progressively weaker signals; this is a discrete multiscale approximation, not an adaptive wavelet/octree or a continuous PDE solution. 2264 × 7 × 3 × 4 = 190176 bytes for residual values. Values clamped and separate from integer money. Seeded event clock: 0.5-second tick, field every four ticks, stochastic event proposal every two ticks, max 8 active and 12 pending. Fixed tick accumulator with bounded catch-up and hidden-page pause; long stalls slow simulated time. Shop weights are derived from field and proximity, event family weights use all seven components plus five Vitra controls. A sale has a six-second temporary event; a small chance of abstract NPC property dispute during it, with pooled receipts and visible reaction. Descendants limited in depth and queue, deferred if occupied. History ring 256 records; receipt chain separate. No targeting based on faith, culture or clothing. The city is statistical; no per-NPC inventory. Sale, spectacle, dispute, repair, replenishment and restitution mapped to temporary actor poses, tray masks and notice plaques. Existing 84 extra models and 21 original imported tee wearers preserved; transient roles are additional lightweight stage figures. This is a first visual implementation, not naturalistic character choreography. Named lore families reuse Ninth Slice, Cup Without a Saucer, Books Nobody Finished, Peach & Peril and others via category descriptions. No multi-stage authored mysteries yet. The Wheel phase follows simulated commercial activity and its lantern palette/intensity reflects aggregate restoration and attention. Audio gain reflects market activity; audio runtime not tested. Vitra controls local numerical biases; no arbitrary authority or direct ledger mutation.

## Exact local accounting
`receipt-chain.js` implements synchronous SHA-256 (verified against Node crypto), sequence IDs, previous receipt hash, positive safe integer transfer validation and conservation between player, circulation, shops, player disputed and NPC disputed pooled accounts. NPC sales move 1 local chip, witnessed disputes 2, optional player disputes 12, restitution reverses the corresponding claim. 10100 initialized chips, no mint in runtime. Gringotts remains an independent reference; no real network, consensus, signature, proof-of-work, or bank settlement. A local save owner can rewrite the entire state/chain; hashes detect accidental edits and inconsistent receipts, not a privileged attacker. IndexedDB when available, portable JSON export/import when blocked. Current save includes aggregate field, receipts, claims and stock, not player identity or character inventory beyond local balance. No multi-tab synchronization, cross-browser authority or authenticated persistent ledger. The preview browser opaque origin blocked IndexedDB; Node roundtrip verified. Explicit reset of navigation does not clear the local economic save.
