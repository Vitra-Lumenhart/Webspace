# The Thousand Chances · TC-R0001 — The Balcony of Arrivals
A self-contained original WebGL2 urban cavern blockout. Open `index.html` on a WebGL2 browser; drag to look, choose local route buttons or W/S and A/D. No Canvas2D fallback. Start in the short entry tube, visit Last Lantern and Balcony, descend into the market, or find the return tube. In the market select fictional purse practice, choose half-step / complete / retreat / guard / leave. A pressure tag is a fictional practice loss only; no transfer or bank effect. The separate district is inspired by the bustling, plural street life of Mos Eisley, not copied assets. Gringotts is an external comparison and optional future service; no connection to its ledger or credentials. Compare https://github.com/Vitra-Lumenhart/Webspace/tree/main/worlds/gringotts and https://docs.google.com/document/d/1L5X6-W1wnZ9j2hz3vbvbx6_9p93WCMJIgw7epVxRbgw/edit .

## Canon & art
All twelve principal sets named, with seven additional route anchors. The large open volume, suspended 18 m wheel, waterfall, quays, three housing tiers, 72 seeded façades, stalls and static inhabitant silhouettes are a first set blockout. Actual scenes are stylized parametric geometry, not final AAA art. Proposed 180×110 m cavern proportions; no abundant Underdark tubes, no modification to Belowroads' existing puzzle. The entry and return sleeves are original short cave-like placements, not integrated UnderDark SDF or portal travel yet. The market's open circulation is authored independently. The twelve-ring wheel has moving markers/spokes; no actual clockwork, water simulation or opportunity service. No downloadable external art.
