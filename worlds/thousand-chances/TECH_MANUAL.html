The Thousand Chances · TC-R0001

Original WebGL2 district; public route atlas, authored open-volume placement, deterministic façades and bounded Xenos-derived practice. Enter · README · External comparison: Gringotts.

Geometry and scene

Right-handed Cartesian meters; Y up. Broad irregular 96-segment, 11-ring shell and market floor. Short tube sleeves at z≈76 and −75; neither actual Belowroads SDF nor connected portal. 19 stable nodes, 27 undirected edges; fixed camera anchor coordinates with eye height 1.7 m, eased interpolation, drag yaw/pitch, W/S nearest forward/backward route, A/D turn, route buttons and atlas. Ground route platforms omitted where broad floor suffices; scenery avoidance heuristic for stalls/silhouettes, not physical collision proof. 72 seeded building records on two walls, three tiers; primitive facades, roofs, windows, timber supports, market stalls, waterwheel, waterfall, 12 concentric rings with moving markers. Static expanded interleaved xyz/normal/rgba float32 vertices; one static draw and dynamic wheel draw; shader directional and illustrative emissive/street-light approximation, fog; no real shadow maps, water dynamics, textures or LOD. Native geometry export for checking renderer source. All artwork generated originally.

Movement and fictional adversary

Ordinary navigation is nodal, not free walk. Xenos-derived conceptual contract (prior Purgatorio discussion, original source unavailable): a temporary local encounter frame, player commitment triggers one rival half-step or guard/wait, symmetric half-edge budgets, explicit half-to-full anchor transfer and retreat. Rival pursues on graph; occupied adjacent anticipated node blocked except designated escape anchors; alternative ranks legal neighbors by remaining BFS distance then local edge length. Proximity radius 1–3 graph edges is diagnostic, not yet physically contracting geometry. Explicit leave always legal, no trap. Four unguarded close-pressure ticks can cause fictional tag; safe anchor transfer evades; 12-round timeout ends practice. Purse ownership stays player throughout; no real theft or external service. Rival is atlas marker, not a 3D animated actor. UI actions do not alter other worlds.

Accretion and canonical art

Generator uses deterministic seed for 72 façade records, epoch/tier and kind labels, not yet a generational social simulation or support solver. Full seven layers, three graphs, six species, historical epochs, attraction fields, alternative niches, cinematic validation and persistent cast are recorded in Accretion contract; production bible. The twelve named sets have stations/lore; no claim of completed interiors. Gringotts is designed order and verified settlement; Thousand is emergent order and alternative value. No integration or financial authority.
